## Documented discovery constraints — 2026-11-03

**SStats.net:** [public API introduction](https://sstats.net/api), [OpenAPI docs](https://api.sstats.net/docs/) and the [public Russia Premier League 2022/23 archive](https://sstats.net/t/235/2022) establish that this provider advertises historical RPL data, free documented `GET /Leagues` and `GET /Games/list` routes, xG/statistics/odds features, and a limit of **30 unauthenticated requests/minute per IP**. They do *not* establish API-level match-by-match RPL coverage, quote capture timestamps, independent rights for private ML training or an approved identity crosswalk. Do not infer that a website route number equals an API league identifier.

**Open-Meteo:** [free noncommercial terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) cap use at fewer than **10,000 requests/day** (5,000/hour, 600/minute) and require CC BY 4.0 attribution. [Previous Runs](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/previous-runs-api) provides fixed-lead forecasts (e.g. `temperature_2m_previous_day1` for a value forecast 24 hours before its valid hour), with most models archived from January 2024 and exceptions back to 2018 or March 2021. [Historical Forecast](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api) stitches the first hours of changing model runs and is **not** itself an exact historical forecast issued at our match-day cutoff. [Single Runs](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api) preserves a specific model initialisation time, but most model archives began in April 2026 (ECMWF earlier). Retrospective match weather requires verified original venue coordinates, a compatible historical forecast model and a cutoff before which the chosen forecast was issued; reanalysis cannot be silently backdated.

**Executable observations:** one protected [workflow](../.github/workflows/m2-enrichment-source-discovery.yml) adds two manual owner-only commands to issue #182: `/m2-sstats-catalog` (at most **two** documented anonymous GETs: league catalog, then one 2022/23 season page only if a **unique** Russian Premier League ID is established from that catalog), and `/m2-weather-previous-day` (one fixed-lead weather hour-series at **reference city coordinates**, not a football venue). Both emit counts and SHA-256 only; they do not retain raw provider data, access 2025/26, approve ML training or attach weather to a match. Failed/ambiguous source responses are **INCONCLUSIVE**, never evidence of absence.
